## Capability summary

This slice covers watcher intake, durable scope, scheduled admission, indexing outcomes, and publication-based recovery. All bullets describe what the tests exercise or expect. They do not establish that the implementations satisfy those expectations.

- [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_controller_intake.py](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_controller_intake.py) exercises classification into VAULT/CODE/DOCUMENT scopes; persistence before legacy dirty-slot acknowledgement; typed preflight refusal; controller reevaluation from service measurements; job creation/dispatch failure cleanup and ownership; durable refusal across new observations, reevaluation, and controller recreation; and preserving a newer rebuild refusal after a no-op success. Its visible collaborators are `watcher_intake`, `watcher_execution`, `watcher_retry`, `watcher_retry_policy`, `watcher_runtime`, `watcher_controller`, server watcher measurements, `ServiceRegistry`, and job manager types. Test ranges: 78–156, 158–236, 274–330, 333–376, 397–530, 531–666.
- [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_durable_scope.py](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_durable_scope.py) exercises observation persistence, atomic admission of an exact captured scope with a job fence, restoration of captured scope on interrupted/failed settlement, and consumption of only the captured generation on success. It directly imports the `watcher_durability` API. Test range: 33–148.
- [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_filter_offload.py](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_filter_offload.py) exercises event-loop responsiveness while filtering, classification, and ignore-policy reads wait on worker-thread barriers; raw watch batches reaching the application filter; old-policy prefilter/new-policy classification ordering on control-file changes; source routing and deleted-file ownership; rejection of ignored deletions before ownership lookup; and rejection of foreign event kinds. Test ranges: 127–209, 211–341, 343–427.
- [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_index_resilience.py](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_index_resilience.py) builds small CPU fixtures around code/document/vault indexers and exercises managed watcher attempts, real index checkpoints, retry/backoff projection, resource release, missing checkpoint telemetry, unchanged work, cancellation, and isolation of VAULT completion from code/document projection. The test substitutes the document encoder and explicitly asserts that checkpoint projection failure does not replace the watcher result. Test ranges: 62–115, 117–218, 221–371.
- [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_load.py](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_load.py) exercises the production `_WatcherScheduler` interface with fake time and controllers: adaptive coalescing/freshness, backpressure, fair rotation, recovery after load, and visible storage-pressure breach without admission. Test range: 109–231.
- [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_measurements.py](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_measurements.py) exercises `compose_watcher_measurement`: mapping supplied service facts, preserving missing inputs as explicit unavailable fields, and immutability. Test range: 18–110.
- [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_publication_certification.py](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_publication_certification.py) exercises fail-closed rebuild reconciliation when job success lacks or contradicts durable publication evidence; prevention of legacy document extraction failures settling refusal; restart handling for historical source attempts; code no-op behavior; production dispatch rejection of failed source checkpoints and missing current owners; and reconciliation of old false-success records with certified rebuilds and newer retry evidence. Test ranges: 86–158, 161–355, 359–547, 549–772, 774–999.
- [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_quiesce_intake.py](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_quiesce_intake.py) exercises dispatch at a closing pause/start boundary and expects the created watcher job, exact dirty paths, and retry claim to remain deferred while job resources are released. Test range: 45–137.

## Interfaces and data flow

- **Observed test seam:** `watcher_intake.watch_and_reindex(configuration)` is called with a `WatcherConfiguration`; tests replace `RustNotify`, binding initialization, and server scheduler callbacks. Batches are filtered/classified into `_WatcherEventBatch` values and persisted through the watcher observation path. The test setup exposes `WatcherChangeRouting`, per-source `_ControllerBinding`s, `WatcherConvergenceSlot`s, `WatcherRetryPolicy`, and `ServiceRegistry`. See [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_filter_offload.py:72–124, 127–209](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_filter_offload.py#L72) and [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_controller_intake.py:89–156](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_controller_intake.py#L89). Exact production ordering and threading remain implementation questions.
- **Scope and retry state:** tests construct `WatcherPathObservation` with relative path, source, timestamps, event kinds, and generation. They inspect retry state fields including pending/captured paths, attempt job/generation/token, convergence-pending, refusal, failure, and circuit state. Durability calls shown are `persist_watcher_observations`, `admit_scoped_watcher_attempt`, and `settle_watcher_attempt`; tests expect an admission decision and cancellation indication, with interrupted/failed work restored and success consuming only its captured generation. See [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_durable_scope.py:48–148](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_durable_scope.py#L48) and [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_controller_intake.py:110–156, 574–616](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_controller_intake.py#L110).
- **Controller and scheduling:** tests provide controller snapshots/scopes and `ControllerMeasurement`s; scheduler entries receive `reevaluate` and `admit` callbacks. Measurement tests build `WatcherMeasurementFacts` from job, limiter, search, pressure, storage, quiesce, retry, and cost inputs. This establishes the exercised interface shape, not the scheduler algorithm or actual fact collection. See [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_load.py:40–107, 119–231](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_load.py#L40) and [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_measurements.py:18–110](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_measurements.py#L18).
- **Job and indexing path (inference from test wiring):** tests submit or dispatch a watcher job through a slot and `JobManager`, then inspect settlement or restart reconciliation. Managed indexing receives `ManagedAttemptInputs` and a `JobAttemptContext`; assertions observe indexer checkpoints and job resilience fields (`generation_id`, committed units, terminal outcome, RSS, retry time, and resource flags). Publication tests directly inspect/update SQLite ledger rows through a ledger path and exercise publication snapshots/proofs. The precise production call graph and persistence guarantees need implementation reads. Evidence: [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_controller_intake.py:333–530](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_controller_intake.py#L333), [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_index_resilience.py:139–218, 221–371](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_index_resilience.py#L139), [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_publication_certification.py:132–158, 359–435, 549–688](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_publication_certification.py#L132).
- **Quiesce boundary:** the quiesce test creates a manager sharing the registry's quiesce controller, admits scope, creates/binds a watcher job, begins pause, then calls `_dispatch_created_watcher_job`; assertions inspect paused job state, desired state, resource holds, slot dirty paths, and retry state. This is a tested interaction boundary, not proof of all pause behavior. See [src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_quiesce_intake.py:71–137](../../../../src/vaultspec_rag/tests/test_watcher_quiesce_intake.py#L71).
